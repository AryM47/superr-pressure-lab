How it works

01 · Win probability

Every ball has a state

A model trained on 818 matches estimates the batting side's chance of winning before each ball from runs, wickets, balls left, target and venue.

02 · Pressure

The top 25% of swing

A ball's pressure is how far the next delivery can move that chance. The top quarter are pressure balls; knockouts and must-win games count 1.5 times.

03 · Credit

Above an average peer

Players earn the win probability they add, compared with batters at the same position or bowlers of the same type, in the same phase, against opposition of known strength.

04 · Rating

Shrunk, weighted, tested

Small samples are pulled toward average, recent seasons count more, and the order is the median of 400 resamples, checked against elite opponents for places 7 to 14.
